# Recovery

A displayed proposal needs a separate current confirmation. Type **no** to decline, choose another returned slot, or **reset** to clear the conversation. Reset does not remove appointments already created.

For no match, correct the phone/DOB once; unresolved records or ZIP ambiguity need clinic scheduling staff. For unsupported scope, advice or outage, contact clinic scheduling staff. The assistant does not queue a handoff.

If booking outcome is **unknown**, do not retry. Ask clinic scheduling staff to verify whether an appointment was created. Reset/restart does not resolve uncertainty; process restart loses the volatile duplicate guard.

See [setup](setup.md), [limitations](limitations.md) and the [support recovery details](../internal/scheduling-recovery.md).
